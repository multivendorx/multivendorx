# Vendor Dashboard

## What it does

Once a vendor is approved, they manage everything about their store — products, orders, earnings, support — from their own **Store Dashboard**, a page on the marketplace's own site (not wp-admin). This guide is written for vendors, but marketplace admins should read it too, since it's what you're pointing your vendors toward.

## Why it matters

A vendor shouldn't need access to wp-admin to run their store. The dashboard gives them exactly what they need — their products, their orders, their money — and nothing of the marketplace's own admin surface.

## How to get there

After logging in, an approved vendor is taken straight to their Store Dashboard (rather than WooCommerce's default My Account page). If you land somewhere else, look for a "My Store" or "Vendor Dashboard" link in your account menu — where exactly it appears depends on the marketplace's own site setup.

## What's on the dashboard

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | An overview of your store's activity |
| **Products** | List, add, and manage your products |
| **Coupons** | Create and manage coupons for your store's products |
| **Sales → Orders** | View and process orders for your products |
| **Sales → Refund** | Handle refund requests |
| **Sales → Commissions** | See the commission taken on your sales |
| **Wallet → Transactions** | Your full earnings/payout transaction history |
| **Wallet → Withdrawals** | Request or track a payout |
| **Store Support → Support Tickets** | Messages between you and the marketplace admin |
| **Store Support → Product Questions** | Customer questions about your products |
| **Store Support → Followers** | Customers following your store |
| **Store Support → Customer Reviews** | Reviews left on your store/products |
| **Resources → Documentation** | Help docs for using the dashboard |
| **Resources → Tools** | Store-level utilities |
| **Settings** | Your own store's settings — separate from the marketplace admin's settings |

A marketplace admin can hide some of these (e.g. **Wallet** or **Products**) for a specific store, or marketplace-wide, via **Settings → Store Permissions** — if something listed here is missing from your dashboard, that's most likely why, not a bug.

## Getting paid

See **Wallet → Transactions** for a running history, and **Wallet → Withdrawals** to request a payout once you've cleared the marketplace's minimum payout threshold — see [PAYOUTS](PAYOUTS.md) for how that threshold and schedule work from the admin side.
