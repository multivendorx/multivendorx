# Settings

## What it does

**MultiVendorX → Settings** holds marketplace-wide configuration, grouped into tabs. There are a lot of them — this guide groups them by what they're actually for, rather than listing all thirty alphabetically.

## Why it matters

This is where the marketplace's actual rules live: how vendors sign up, what they're allowed to do, how money moves, and how the whole thing looks and complies with policy. Commissions and Payouts are big enough to have their own guides — everything else is here.

## Vendor sign-up

| Tab | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Onboarding** | The vendor registration/approval flow itself |
| **Registration** | What a new vendor fills in when signing up |

See [GETTING-STARTED](GETTING-STARTED.md) and [STORES](STORES.md) for how this connects to the Approval Queue.

## Vendor access & identity

| Tab | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **User Permissions** | What a vendor's staff/sub-accounts can do |
| **Store Permissions** | What a vendor store itself can do (upload without review, manage shipping, etc.) |
| **Store Identity** | What a vendor can customize about their public store page (name, logo, banner) |
| **Dashboard Menu** | Which sections appear on the vendor dashboard, and in what order |

See [STORES](STORES.md) and [VENDOR-DASHBOARD](VENDOR-DASHBOARD.md).

## Store & products

| Tab | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Store** | General store-level configuration |
| **Products** | Marketplace-wide product rules |
| **Store Inventory** | Stock-related settings across stores |
| **Product Preferences** | Default product behavior |
| **Product Advertising** | Vendor product promotion/advertising options |
| **Product Report Abuse** | Letting customers flag a problematic product listing |
| **Single Product, Multiple Store** | Whether more than one vendor can list the same product |
| **Coupons & Discounts** | Marketplace-wide coupon rules |

## Money

| Tab | What it controls | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Commission Rule**, **Commissions** | How much the marketplace keeps per sale | [COMMISSIONS](COMMISSIONS.md) |
| **Payouts**, **Withdrawal Methods** | How and when vendors get paid | [PAYOUTS](PAYOUTS.md) |
| **Refunds** | Refund handling between customer, vendor, and marketplace |

## Compliance & legal

| Tab | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Legal Compliance** | General legal/compliance configuration |
| **Product Compliance** | Product-specific compliance requirements |
| **Tax Compliance** | Tax handling across vendors |
| **Policies** | Marketplace policy documents vendors must agree to |
| **Privacy** | Privacy-related configuration |

See **MultiVendorX → Compliance** for the admin screen these tabs feed into.

## Appearance & location

| Tab | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Appearance** | Visual customization of marketplace-facing pages |
| **Custom CSS** | Free-text CSS for styling tweaks without a child theme |
| **Geolocation** | Location-based marketplace behavior |
| **Shipping** | Marketplace-wide shipping rules |

## Reviews

| Tab | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Store Reviews** | Whether and how customers can review a vendor store |

## Overview

The **Overview** tab is the Settings page's own landing tab — a general-purpose starting point rather than a specific feature area.
