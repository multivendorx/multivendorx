# Stores

## What it does

**MultiVendorX → Stores** is the full list of every vendor on your marketplace — their status, their lifetime earnings, and quick actions to manage each one.

## Why it matters

This is your central control point for who's actually allowed to sell on your marketplace, and how they're doing. Without reviewing new stores, anyone could start selling the moment they register — the approval workflow exists so you can vet vendors before they go live.

## How to use it

1. Go to **MultiVendorX → Stores**.
2. Filter by status: **All**, **Active**, **Under Review**, **Suspended**, or **Deactivated**.
3. Each row shows the **Store**, its **Lifetime Earning**, and quick actions — jump to that store's own **Settings**, or view its public **Storefront**.
4. Click **Add Store** to create a vendor store manually, rather than waiting for self-registration.

## Approving new vendors

New registrations land under **Under Review** (or **Rejected** if you've declined one). Go to **MultiVendorX → Approval Queue** to review and approve or reject pending vendors and their first products before they go live — see [GETTING-STARTED](GETTING-STARTED.md) for the registration flow itself.

## Store statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Active** | Approved and selling normally |
| **Under Review** | Registered, waiting on your approval |
| **Rejected** | Registration declined |
| **Suspended** | Temporarily blocked from selling — use this instead of deleting a store for a policy violation you want to resolve, not end permanently |
| **Deactivated** | Store no longer active |

## Store permissions

Go to **Settings → Store Permissions** and **Settings → User Permissions** to decide what vendors can do at all — e.g. whether they can upload products without review, manage their own shipping, or need every change approved. Go to **Settings → Store Identity** to control what a vendor can customize about how their store appears publicly (name, logo, banner).
