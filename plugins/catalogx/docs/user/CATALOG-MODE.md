# Catalog Mode

## What it does

Catalog mode hides WooCommerce's normal buying flow on the products you choose: the price can be hidden, and the "Add to Cart" button is replaced or removed. The product is still visible, searchable, and browsable — customers just can't buy it directly.

## Why it matters

Some products or stores aren't meant for direct online purchase: wholesale catalogs, B2B lines, made-to-order items, or regions where a product can't be sold online. Catalog mode lets you keep your full WooCommerce product catalog as the single source of truth, without building a separate "lookbook" site.

## How to use it

1. Go to **CatalogX → Modules** and confirm the **Catalog** module is active.
2. Go to **Settings → Enquiry & Quote → Store experience** and choose **Browse products only** to apply catalog mode store-wide, or leave it as **Browse & purchase products** and instead restrict catalog mode to specific products/roles.
3. To scope catalog mode to specific products, roles, categories, tags or brands instead of the whole store, use **Settings → Access Rules** — see [SETTINGS](SETTINGS.md#access-rules).
4. Visit an affected product on the storefront: the price and/or Add to Cart button should be gone.

## What customers see instead

With Add to Cart removed, a product typically shows an **Enquiry** button, a **Quote** button, both, or neither — whichever modules you've enabled and the product qualifies for. See [ENQUIRY](ENQUIRY.md) and [QUOTES](QUOTES.md).
