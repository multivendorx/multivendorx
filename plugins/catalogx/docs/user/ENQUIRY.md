# Enquiry

## What it does

Enquiry adds an "ask about this product" button to your shop and product pages. Instead of buying, a customer fills in a short form; you receive it and can follow up directly.

## Why it matters

Not every sale starts with "add to cart" — custom quantities, bespoke products, or anything where price depends on a conversation benefits from a direct line to the customer instead of forcing them through checkout (or away from your site entirely).

## How to use it

1. Go to **CatalogX → Modules** and confirm the **Enquiry** module is active.
2. The enquiry button appears automatically on the shop page and each product page for any product catalog mode applies to. You can also place it manually anywhere using the `[catalogx_enquiry_button]` shortcode.
3. Go to **Settings → Enquiry & Quote** to control who can enquire, what happens after someone submits a form, and where enquiries are sent.
4. A customer clicks the button, fills in the form, and submits — you're notified at the recipient email you configured.

## Settings explained

Go to **Settings → Enquiry & Quote**.

| Setting | What it does | Why you'd change it |
|---|---|---|
| **Store experience** | Browse-only catalog vs. browse-and-purchase | Decide whether enquiry is the *only* path to a sale, or an alternative |
| **Who can enquire** | Everyone, or logged-in customers only | Restrict enquiries to registered accounts if you want to filter out anonymous spam |
| **Show enquiry for** | All products, or out-of-stock products only | Use enquiry as a waitlist-style fallback, or offer it everywhere |
| **Display enquiry form as** | Popup or inline | Match your theme's style |
| **What happens after submission** | Stay on the page, or redirect to another page | A dedicated "thank you" page can carry tracking/confirmation messaging |
| **Recipient email for product enquiries** | Where enquiry submissions are sent | Point it at whoever actually handles sales conversations |
| **Enable WhatsApp enquiries** [PRO] | Adds a WhatsApp contact option alongside the form | Faster back-and-forth for customers who prefer chat |

## What's Pro

- A dedicated **Enquiry Messages** admin screen to view and reply to conversations in one place, instead of just getting an email. **[PRO]**
- WhatsApp enquiries, with a configurable contact number and message template. **[PRO]**
- A drag-and-drop form builder for the enquiry form (the free version uses a fixed form). **[PRO]**
- Customizable enquiry email templates. **[PRO]**
