# Settings

## What it does

**CatalogX → Settings** holds every configuration option, split across several tabs: **Pages & Shortcodes**, **Wholesale**, **Access Rules**, **System**, **Enquiry & Quote**, **Email Customizer**, and **Form Builder**.

## Why it matters

This is where you decide which pages host which feature, who can see or use catalog/enquiry/quote, and how every message and form looks. The defaults work out of the box, but a few minutes here makes it match your store's actual sales process.

## Pages & Shortcodes

Go to **Settings → Pages & Shortcodes**.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Set enquiry cart page** [PRO] | The page that hosts the enquiry cart flow |
| **Set request quote page** [PRO] | The page that hosts the quote request flow |
| **Set Wholesale Products Page** [PRO] | The page that hosts the wholesale product catalog |

All three page-assignment fields on this tab are Pro-gated — the free plugin uses sensible built-in defaults for where each flow lives.

This tab also lists every shortcode CatalogX provides, so you can place buttons and forms manually in a page or post.

## Wholesale — see [WHOLESALE-AND-RULES](WHOLESALE-AND-RULES.md#wholesale--pro)

Almost entirely **[PRO]** — sign-up mode, coupon interaction, offer visibility, catalog mode, and pricing mode.

## Access Rules

Go to **Settings → Access Rules**. This is how you scope catalog mode, enquiry, or quote to less than your whole store — by **User Role**, **User Name**, **Product**, **Category**, **Tag**, or **Brand**, separately for **Catalog**, **Enquiry**, and **Quote**.

Use this when, for example, you want catalog mode active for everyone *except* wholesale customers, or you only want the enquiry button on a handful of custom-order products rather than your whole catalog.

## System

Go to **Settings → System**. General system settings, plus a free-text **Additional CSS** box for styling tweaks without a child theme.

## Enquiry & Quote

Go to **Settings → Enquiry & Quote**. Covered in detail in [ENQUIRY](ENQUIRY.md) and [QUOTES](QUOTES.md) — this tab controls your store's overall catalog/buy mode, who can enquire or request a quote, form behavior, where enquiries are sent, WhatsApp enquiries, quote validity, and PDF delivery.

## Email Customizer — [PRO]

Three sub-tabs (Enquiry, Quote, Wholesale) for customizing the actual email templates sent for each feature. The free plugin sends sensible default emails; customizing their content and design is a Pro feature.

## Form Builder — [PRO]

Two sub-tabs. **Enquiry** shows a preview of the free plugin's fixed enquiry form; a full drag-and-drop field builder is Pro. **Wholesale Registration** is the form customers fill in to apply for a wholesale account — building it is a Pro feature.
