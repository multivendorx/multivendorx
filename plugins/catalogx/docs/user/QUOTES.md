# Quotes

## What it does

Quote lets a customer add one or more products to a quote cart — separate from the normal WooCommerce cart — and submit a request-a-quote. You review it under **Quotation Requests** and respond with pricing.

## Why it matters

Bulk orders, custom pricing, and B2B deals often can't be priced with a single "add to cart" click. A quote request gives you the customer's intent and product list up front, so you can price the whole order properly before any money changes hands.

## How to use it

1. Go to **CatalogX → Modules** and confirm the **Quote** module is active.
2. The quote button appears on eligible products (controlled the same way as enquiry — see [SETTINGS](SETTINGS.md)). A customer can add several products to their quote cart before submitting one combined request.
3. Go to **CatalogX → Quotation Requests** to see every request that's come in.
4. Each row shows the order's products and customer, its **Total**, and its **Status**. Select rows and use **Download CSV** to export them.
5. Respond to the customer directly (by email, or using **Send Mail** / **View** on Pro) with your pricing.

## Settings explained

Go to **Settings → Enquiry & Quote → Requests**.

| Setting | What it does | Why you'd change it |
|---|---|---|
| **Who can request a quote** | Everyone, or logged-in users only | Restrict to registered customers if you want quote requests tied to accounts |
| **Quotation validity period** | Lifetime, or a fixed duration | A fixed expiry pushes customers to decide rather than letting a quote sit indefinitely |
| **PDF delivery mode** [PRO] | Available as a download, or attached to the customer's email | Choose however your customers expect to receive a formal quote document |

Choosing which page hosts the quote request flow (**Settings → Pages & Shortcodes → Set request quote page**) is a **[PRO]** setting — the free plugin uses a built-in default page.

## What's Pro

- Viewing and replying to a quote request directly from the admin table (**View** / **Send Mail**). **[PRO]**
- Generating a PDF quote document and choosing how it's delivered. **[PRO]**
