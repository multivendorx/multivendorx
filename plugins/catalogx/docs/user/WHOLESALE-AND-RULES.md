# Wholesale & Dynamic Pricing Rules

## What it does

Two related, mostly-Pro features for B2B selling: **Wholesale** gives specific customers a separate pricing tier and registration flow; **Dynamic Pricing Rules** applies pricing rules automatically by role, quantity, or product.

## Why it matters

If you sell to both retail and business customers, you usually don't want them to see the same prices or the same catalog. Wholesale lets you register and approve B2B customers with their own pricing; Rules lets that pricing adjust automatically instead of you manually discounting every order.

## Wholesale — [PRO]

The **Wholesale Users** admin screen and the **Wholesale** settings tab exist in the free plugin, but are upgrade prompts until Pro is active. Once on Pro, you can:

- Let customers sign up for a wholesale account manually or automatically.
- Choose whether wholesale discounts stack with coupons.
- Keep wholesale pricing private, or promote it openly.
- Run wholesale as a separate catalog, or blended into your normal shop.
- Price each product individually, or apply one blanket discount to everything.

Set which page hosts wholesale product browsing under **Settings → Pages & Shortcodes → Set Wholesale Products Page**, and build the registration form itself under **Settings → Form Builder → Wholesale Registration**.

## Dynamic Pricing Rules — [PRO]

The **Dynamic Pricing Rules** admin screen exists in the free plugin as an upgrade prompt. On Pro, it lets you define rules — e.g. "10% off for the Wholesale role on orders of 50+ units" — that apply automatically at checkout without you touching each product's price.
