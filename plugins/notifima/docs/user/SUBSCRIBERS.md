# Subscribers

## What it does

**Notifima → Subscriber List** shows everyone who's signed up for a restock notification: which product, which email (and phone, if collected), and when they subscribed. You can filter it and download it as a CSV.

## Why it matters

This list is both your notification queue and a demand signal — a product with a long subscriber list is a product worth restocking sooner. It's also where you go if a customer asks "did my signup go through?" or you need to manually remove someone.

## How to use it

1. Open **Notifima → Subscriber List**.
2. Use the category filters at the top to narrow the list (e.g. by date range).
3. Each row shows the **Product**, its **SKU**, the subscriber's **Email** and **Phone** (if your form collects it), and the **Date** they subscribed.
4. Select rows and use **Download CSV** to export them — useful for backing up your list or importing it elsewhere.

## Removing subscribers

You don't need to come here to remove subscribers for a product you're discontinuing: on the regular WooCommerce **Products** list, select the product(s), choose the **Remove Subscribers** bulk action, and apply it. This clears every subscriber for those products in one step.

## What's Pro

- Exporting full subscriber detail (names, subscription dates, product names and emails) in bulk. **[PRO]**
- Letting customers unsubscribe themselves from their My Account page. **[PRO]**
- Blocking subscriptions from specific email addresses or domains. **[PRO]**
