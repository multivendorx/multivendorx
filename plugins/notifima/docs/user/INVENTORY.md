# Inventory Manager

## What it does

**Notifima → Inventory Manager** lists every product in your store with its stock details in one table — price, stock status, backorder setting and quantity — instead of opening each product individually.

## Why it matters

Checking stock product-by-product in WooCommerce's own Products screen is slow once your catalog grows. This gives you one screen to scan for what's low, what's out, and what needs attention, right next to the waitlist data that tells you how much demand is waiting on each one.

## How to use it

1. Open **Notifima → Inventory Manager**.
2. Use **Select Product Type** to filter to **Simple** or **Variable** products.
3. Each row shows the **Product**, **SKU**, **Regular Price**, **Sale Price**, whether **Manage Stock** is on, **Stock Status**, whether **Backorders** are allowed, and the current **Stock** quantity.
4. Select rows and use **Download CSV** to export the list.

## What's Pro

- Editing stock, SKU and other fields inline, right in this table, without opening each product. **[PRO]**
- Exporting your full stock data, editing it, and importing it back via CSV. **[PRO]**
