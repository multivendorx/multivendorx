# Dashboard

## What it does

**Notifima → Dashboard** is your landing page when you open the plugin. It has two tabs: **Dashboard**, a general overview and cross-promotion area, and **Free vs Pro**, a side-by-side feature comparison (hidden automatically once you're on Pro).

## Why it matters

It's the fastest way to confirm Notifima is active and to see, at a glance, what you get by upgrading if you're still on the free version.

## How to use it

1. Open **Notifima → Dashboard**.
2. The **Dashboard** tab shows an upgrade prompt (free version only) and highlights the other plugins in the MultiVendorX family (CatalogX, MultiVendorX) in case they're useful for your store.
3. Switch to the **Free vs Pro** tab to compare what each tier includes before deciding whether to upgrade.

## Settings explained

There are no settings on this screen — it's informational only. Head to [SETTINGS](SETTINGS.md) to actually configure the plugin.
